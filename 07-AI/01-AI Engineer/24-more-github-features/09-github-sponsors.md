## Summary
GitHub Sponsors allows the community to financially support the developers and organizations who build and maintain the open-source software they rely on. It's a way to ensure the sustainability of the open-source ecosystem.

## Detailed Explanation

### How it Works
- **Sponsorship Tiers**: Developers can set up monthly or one-time payment tiers with different rewards.
- **Zero Fees**: GitHub currently covers all payment processing fees, meaning 100% of the sponsorship goes to the developer.
- **Eligibility**: Available to individual contributors and organizations in supported regions.

### Relevance to AI
Many of the core libraries AI Engineers use (like `scikit-learn`, `pandas`, or niche LLM utilities) are maintained by small teams or individuals. Sponsoring these projects helps ensure they continue to be maintained and secured.

## Interview Questions

**Q: What is the difference between a one-time and a monthly sponsorship?**
**A:** A monthly sponsorship provides recurring support, which is better for a developer's long-term sustainability. A one-time sponsorship is a "tip" for a specific contribution or feature.

**Q: Can organizations sponsor developers?**
**A:** Yes, many companies sponsor the open-source projects they use in production to ensure those projects stay healthy.
