## Summary
The quality of a fine-tuned model is directly proportional to the quality of its training data ("Garbage In, Garbage Out"). Dataset preparation involves collecting, cleaning, and formatting data into specific structures like Instruction Tuning or Chat formats.

## Detailed Explanation
### **Dataset Formats**
1.  **Instruction Tuning (Prompt-Completion)**:
    *   `{"prompt": "Calculate the square root of 144", "completion": "12"}`
2.  **Chat Format (User-Assistant)**:
    *   `{"messages": [{"role": "system", "content": "You are a helpful assistant."}, {"role": "user", "content": "Hello!"}, {"role": "assistant", "content": "Hi there!"}]}`
    *   This is now the standard for fine-tuning models like Llama 3 or GPT-4o-mini.

### **Data Cleaning & Diversity**
-   **Deduplication**: Removing near-identical examples to prevent the model from over-fitting.
-   **Synthetic Data**: Using a stronger model (e.g., GPT-4o) to generate training examples for a smaller model. This is common for reasoning or coding tasks.
-   **Instruction Diversity**: Ensuring the model sees many ways of asking the same question to improve generalization.

### **Tokens and Context Window**
During preparation, you must ensure that your examples fit within the model's maximum context window (e.g., 8k, 32k, 128k tokens). Long examples may need to be truncated or split.

## Interview Questions
*   **Q: What is "Synthetic Data" and why is it used in fine-tuning?**
    *   **A:** Synthetic data is training data generated by another AI model rather than humans. It is used because it's cheaper, faster to produce at scale, and can be used to "distill" the capabilities of a large, expensive model into a smaller, more efficient one.
*   **Q: How do you handle "Instruction Drift" during dataset preparation?**
    *   **A:** By ensuring a diverse set of instructions and occasionally mixing in a percentage of the model's original pre-training data (often called "replay buffer") to maintain its general-purpose capabilities.
*   **Q: Why is deduplication important in a fine-tuning dataset?**
    *   **A:** If the model sees the same example too many times, it will memorize it (over-fitting) rather than learning the underlying pattern, which leads to poor performance on new, unseen queries.
