## Summary
The **Roadmap View** in GitHub Projects provides a timeline-based visualization of your work. It's essential for AI Engineers to plan long-term research goals, release cycles, and model training schedules.

## Detailed Explanation
### **Visualizing Time**
- **Start and End Dates**: You can assign specific dates to items to see them on a timeline.
- **Iterations**: Group work into week-long or month-long blocks.
- **Milestones**: Link items to specific project milestones (e.g., "v1.0 Release", "Paper Submission").

### **Roadmapping for AI**
- **Model Training Windows**: Visualizing how long a large model training run is expected to take.
- **Feature Rollouts**: Planning when specific AI features (like a new chatbot interface) will be released to users.
- **Research Phases**: Mapping out the discovery, experimentation, and productionization phases of a new AI capability.

## Interview Questions
**Q: When would you use a Roadmap view instead of a Kanban board?**
**A:** Use a **Roadmap** when you need to visualize the timing and duration of tasks, understand dependencies over time, and plan for specific deadlines or release windows. Use **Kanban** for managing the daily flow of individual tasks.

**Q: How do "Iterations" help in planning AI development?**
**A:** Iterations allow a team to plan work in fixed time blocks (sprints). This is useful for AI teams to set specific goals for each block, such as "Improve model accuracy by 2% this week," providing a clear rhythm for the project.
