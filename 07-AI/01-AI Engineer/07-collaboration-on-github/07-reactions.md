## Summary
**Reactions** (emojis like 👍, 🎉, ❤️) allow users to respond to comments without adding extra "noise" to the discussion thread.

## Detailed Explanation
In busy AI repositories, reactions are vital for:
- **Voting**: Using 👍/👎 on a feature request to gauge community interest (e.g., "Should we add support for JAX?").
- **Acknowledgment**: Reacting with 👀 to show you've seen a bug report and are looking into it.
- **Celebration**: Using 🎉 or 🚀 when a major model is merged or a difficult bug is fixed.

### **Why they matter**
They prevent "Me too!" or "+1" comments, which clutter the timeline and send unnecessary email notifications to everyone subscribed to the issue.

## Interview Questions
**Q: Why are reactions preferred over "+1" comments?**
**A:** "+1" comments trigger email and web notifications for every subscriber, which can be disruptive in large projects. Reactions provide the same signal without the notification spam.

**Q: How can project maintainers use reactions to prioritize work?**
**A:** Maintainers can sort issues by the number of 👍 reactions to identify the most requested features or most impactful bugs.
