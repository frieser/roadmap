## Summary
**Saved Replies** are snippets of text that you can reuse across Issues and Pull Requests to save time. They are particularly useful for senior AI Engineers who frequently give the same feedback or request the same information.

## Detailed Explanation
### **Use Cases for AI Engineers**
- **Requesting Environment Info**: "Please provide your `torch.collect_env()` output and GPU model."
- **PR Review Feedback**: "Great change! Could you please provide a loss curve or benchmark results to verify this doesn't degrade performance?"
- **Closing Duplicate Issues**: "This seems to be a duplicate of #456. Please follow the discussion there."
- **Policy Reminders**: "Thank you for the contribution! Please ensure you have signed the CLA and updated the tests."

### **How to use**
1. Go to your **Personal Settings** -> **Saved Replies**.
2. Create a reply with a title and content.
3. In any comment box, click the "Saved replies" icon or use the shortcut (often `Ctrl + .`) to select one.

## Interview Questions
**Q: How do Saved Replies help in maintaining a consistent tone in open-source AI projects?**
**A:** They allow maintainers to craft polite, helpful, and standardized responses for common scenarios, ensuring that every contributor gets a high-quality response regardless of which team member handles the issue.
