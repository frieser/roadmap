# Collaborators vs Members

## Summary
*   **Outside Collaborator**: Someone invited to a specific repository but not part of the Organization.
*   **Member**: A user who is part of the Organization. They can be added to Teams and given access to multiple repositories at once.

## Detailed Explanation

### Use Case
*   **Collaborator**: Freelancer working on one project.
*   **Member**: Employee of the company.

### Billing
On paid plans, you are usually billed for each Member and Outside Collaborator who has access to private repositories.

## Interview Questions
**Q: Can a member be an admin?**
**A:** Yes, members can have the "Owner" role in the organization, giving them full control.
